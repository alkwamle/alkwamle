### What I'm building

**Kwamle Media** is an AI social media management and selling platform for small businesses, online stores and agencies:

- **Publish** posts, Reels and videos to Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Threads and Telegram from one calendar.
- **Reply** to comments and messages in the customer's own language and in the brand's voice, with a unified team inbox and human takeover.
- **Sell** inside the chat: product cards, order confirmation on WhatsApp, and a store on the customer's own domain.
- **Bring your own AI keys** (OpenAI, Gemini, Claude) and control spend per workspace.
- **13 languages**, an Android app for support teams, and a public API.
